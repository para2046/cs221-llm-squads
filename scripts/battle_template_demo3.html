Watch it in StarCraft II

Every game here ran on the real StarCraft II engine (version 4.10, headless on the cluster). The two Gemma 4 E4B games (untrained and after SFT on 31B games) were saved as standard .SC2Replay files; the two Gemma 4 31B games were not recorded. Open the replays in the game client to see the battle with the full game graphics, camera and sound.

  1. Install StarCraft II from Battle.net (free to play) and start it once.
  2. Copy 32x32_flat.SC2Map and 32x32_flat_2p.SC2Map from the zip into StarCraft II/Maps/SMAC_Maps/, creating the folders if needed.
  3. Double-click a .SC2Replay file, or use Replays → Browse in the game. If the client asks for game version 4.10, let it download.

Both squads use SMAC's custom "_RL" variants of Stalkers, Zealots and Colossi, defined in the map file, so they look like the normal units in game.
